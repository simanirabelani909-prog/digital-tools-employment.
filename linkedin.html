Building a Professional Online Presence

A professional online profile allows students and graduates to share their education, skills, experience and career interests with potential employers. It can also help them connect with professionals and find job opportunities.

LinkedIn is a professional networking platform that users can use to build their profiles, follow organisations, connect with others and explore jobs.

LEARN MORE ABOUT LINKEDIN

What Is LinkedIn?

Online professional networking platform where people can create profiles, share professional information, connect with others and explore career opportunities. Students can use it to start building their professional presence before graduating.

A well-prepared profile can help students communicate their qualifications, skills, projects and career interests. It can also provide a place to showcase achievements and connect with people in their fields.

Why Should Students Use LinkedIn?

1. Share your Qualifications

Students can include their educational background, qualifications, relevant coursework, projects and achievements. This helps visitors understand their areas of study and interests.

2. Write your skills

A profile can highlight technical and professional skills, including computer literacy, communication, teamwork and other abilities relevant to the student's career goals.

3. Build Professional Connections

Students can connect with classmates, lecturers, former students, recruiters and professionals. These connections can help them learn about different career paths and industries.

4. Search Career Opportunities

You can follow Companies, explore LinkedIn Jobs and learn about vacancies, internships and graduate opportunities that match yourinterests.

How to Create a Professional LinkedIn Profile

The following steps can help students create a profile that presents their professional information clearly.

  1. Use your real name. Make sure your name is clear and professional.
  2. Add a suitable profile photograph. Choose an appropriate, clear image that represents you professionally.
  3. Write a professional headline. Briefly explain your field of study, skills or career interests.
  4. Complete your education section. Include your institution, qualification and relevant educational achievements.
  5. Describe your skills and experience. Include relevant projects, tutoring, volunteering, leadership activities, work experience and technical skills where applicable.
  6. Write an informative About section. Summarise your background, interests and the professional skills you are developing.
  7. Build relevant connections. Connect respectfully with classmates, lecturers, professionals and organisations in your field.
  8. Keep your profile updated. Add new skills, qualifications, projects and achievements as you develop them.

Tips for Maintaining a Professional Profile

Make sure the information on your profile is accurate and reflects your qualifications and experience. Check your spelling and grammar, use professional language and avoid sharing personal or confidential information online.

You do not need years of employment experience to build a professional profile. You can include your education, student leadership , practical experience, skills and willingness to learn.

Start Building Your Professional Network

Creating a professional profile is one way to begin preparing for future employment. Keep your information accurate, connect with people respectfully and explore opportunities that support your career goals.

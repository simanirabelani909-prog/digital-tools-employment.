Digital Tools for Employment Preparation

University students can use digital tools to prepare for employment, develop professional skills and improve their readiness for the workplace. These tools support activities such as creating a curriculum vitae (CV), searching for jobs, building professional networks, learning new skills and preparing for interviews.

Below are eight categories of digital tools that can help students prepare for their careers. These include international tools as well as South African resources for jobseekers graduates and young people looking for employment opportunities.

Digital Tools for Students

01

CV Builders

CV builders help students organise their qualifications, education, skills and work experience into a professional curriculum vitae. Templates can guide students who are creating their first CV or updating an existing one.

Examples: Canva and Zety.

VisitCanva Open Zety
02

Professional Networking

Professional networking helps students build connections with employers, recruiters and professionals. Students can create profiles, showcase their skills, follow South African companies and learn about career opportunities.

Examples: LinkedIn and PNet professional profiles.

VisitLinkedIn Access PNet
03

Job-Search Websites

Job-search websites allow students and graduates to find vacancies, review job requirements and apply for suitable positions. Students can search for graduate programmes, internships and jobs matching their qualifications and skills.

Examples: PNet and Careers24.

Dicover PNet View Careers24
04

Online Portfolios

Online portfolios allow students to showcase projects, assignments, presentations and practical skills. For example, a student can display a website, a research project or examples of completed work to demonstrate their abilities to potential employers.

Examples: Wix and WordPress.

Visit Wix Explore WordPress
05

Online Learning Platforms

Online learning platforms help students develop digital, technical and professional skills. Students can complete learning modules and explore career-related topics to supplement their university studies and prepare for workplace demands.

Examples: Microsoft Learn and Siyavula My Future.

Find Microsoft Learn here VisitSiyavula My Future
06

Online Courses and Certificates

Online courses can help students gain additional knowledge in areas such as technology, business, communication and data skills. Some programmes offer certificates that learners may include on their CVs or professional profiles.

Examples: Google Career Certificates and LinkedIn Learning.

Explore Google Certificates Learn more about LinkedIn Learning
07

Interview Preparation

Interview preparation resources help students practise answering common interview questions, explain their skills and prepare for discussions with employers. Students can also learn how to research an organisation before an interview.

Examples: South African Government job-hunting guidance and Big Interview.

Government Interview Guidance Check Out Big Interview
08

South African Employment Opportunities

South African employment platforms help workseekers explore jobs, learnerships and other opportunities. SA Youth provides access to learning and work opportunities, while ESSA allows workseekers to register and search for opportunities through the Department of Employment and Labour.

Examples: SA Youth and ESSA.

Visit SA Youth Get Started ESSA

Using Digital Tools Effectively

Students can combine different tools according to their career goals. For example, they can use Canva to prepare a CV, LinkedIn to build professional connections, PNet to search for vacancies and online learning platforms to develop additional skills.

Digital tools support career preparation, but students should check the accuracy of their information, protect their personal details and ensure that their applications reflect their genuine qualifications and abilities.
